## Short description

A city's lie, in the hands of three scribes.

## Page text

Words drift. Every generation says them a little differently, and every copy of an old document is a choice about what to keep.

You are a scribe in the city of Mēros. People bring you old writings (door signs, grain receipts, oaths, letters, a chronicle) and ask for a copy in today's tongue. You lay **sound-change cards** on the strip, and every word on the page shifts with them. Some words land on a word that already means something else. Some old meanings no longer exist. Your margin notes, old spellings and erasures are few.

Every copy is scored on two scales:
- **Fidelity:** is the old meaning kept?
- **Clarity:** can today's reader follow?

You can rarely have both. And what you keep, someone later will read.

**Three eras, forty-five jobs, three scribes.** What the first scribe writes down decides what the third one can prove. The chronicle at the top of the desk records your choices; the ending is written from them.

### How to play
- Click (or drag) a sound card onto the strip. Hover a card to preview what it does.
- Scribe cards (margin note, old spelling, erasure) go on a single word: click the card, then the word, or drag it there.
- Press the pen to write the copy, then the seal to deliver.
- **M** mutes the sound. Progress saves automatically.
- Languages: English and Turkish (switch on the title page).

About 60–90 minutes. Runs in desktop browsers.
